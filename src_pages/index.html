A Heritage of Flavor

Born from a deep reverence for India's rich culinary traditions, Spice & Soul is where centuries-old recipes meet the refinement of modern fine dining. Every dish tells a story of spice routes, royal kitchens, and family heirlooms.

Our Story

Direct from the Spice Path

Our spice route begins with direct-trade alliances supporting family-owned spice estates in Kerala and Rajasthan. By sourcing cardamom, saffron, and stone-ground pepper directly, we guarantee pure, vibrant aromatics in every dish.

Our Sourcing

The Art of Tandoor & Fire

Cooked inside traditional hand-crafted clay ovens heated to intense temperatures over burning charcoal, our kebabs, paneers, and flatbreads absorb a signature smoky flavor and perfect texture that modern cooking simply cannot replicate.

Explore Dishes

Sensory Pairings for the Palate

Elevate your dining experience with custom pairings hand-selected by our Master Sommelier. From bold reds that stand up to complex marinades, to spice-infused botanic cocktails, we curate pairings that balance heat with refinement.

Bar Program

Exclusive Chef's Table

Book an intimate, multi-course degustation menu hosted directly by Executive Chef Arjun Kapoor. Experience custom seasonal recipes, live kitchen tours, and direct insights into the historic origin of each flavor profile.
