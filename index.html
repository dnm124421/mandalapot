A Heritage of Flavor

Born from a deep reverence for India's rich culinary traditions, Spice & Soul is where centuries-old recipes meet the refinement of modern fine dining. Every dish tells a story of spice routes, royal kitchens, and family heirlooms.

Our Story

Direct from the Spice Path

Our spice route begins with direct-trade alliances supporting family-owned spice estates in Kerala and Rajasthan. By sourcing cardamom, saffron, and stone-ground pepper directly, we guarantee pure, vibrant aromatics in every dish.

Our Sourcing

The Art of Tandoor & Fire

Cooked inside traditional hand-crafted clay ovens heated to intense temperatures over burning charcoal, our kebabs, paneers, and flatbreads absorb a signature smoky flavor and perfect texture that modern cooking simply cannot replicate.

Our Story

"Food is not just nourishment — it is memory, it is culture, it is love."

Founded in 2008 by Chef Arjun Kapoor, Spice & Soul was born from a singular vision: to honor the magnificent culinary diversity of India in a setting befitting its royal heritage.

What began as a small family recipe has grown into a celebrated dining institution, earning two Michelin stars and the hearts of guests from across the world.

Our Core Pillars

Culinary Philosophy

Pillar One

Farm to Table

Every spice, grain, and herb at Spice & Soul tells a story of the soil it was grown in. We bypass industrial supply chains to partner directly with family-owned spice estates in Kerala and heritage wheat farms in Rajasthan.

  • check_circle
    100% Organic Spices Harvested at the peak of flavor and stone-ground by hand.
  • check_circle
    Ethical Sourcing Direct-trade relations ensuring fair prices and sustainability for farmers.
Pillar Two

Heirloom Recipes

Our kitchen is a sanctuary of time-honored culinary secrets. We do not write down our master spice-blend ratios; they are passed down through oral tradition and intensive apprenticeship, preserving the pure soul of regional Indian home cooking.

  • check_circle
    Ancestral Formulas Four generations of secret family spice mixes (masalas).
  • check_circle
    Traditional Cooking Slow-simmered in copper degchis and roasted in hand-crafted clay tandoors.
Pillar Three

Modern Refinement

While we respect our heritage, we are not bound by it. We blend traditional flavors with modern culinary science, creating multisensory dining experiences that surprise and delight the contemporary global palate.

  • check_circle
    Contemporary Plating Aesthetic presentation designed to captivate visual senses.
  • check_circle
    Multisensory Tasting Balanced textures and temperatures that redefine familiar dishes.

Our Legacy

Our Journey

2008

The Genesis

2008

The Genesis

Chef Arjun Kapoor establishes a modest 30-seat eatery in Mumbai's historic Colaba district. Infusing family secrets with refined cooking techniques, Spice & Soul immediately sets a new benchmark for regional Indian gastronomy.

2012

National Expansion

2012

National Expansion

Expanding our horizon, we open a flagship dining parlor in New Delhi. Designed with Mughal-inspired arches and marble interiors, the Delhi branch cements Spice & Soul's reputation as a national destination for luxury dining.

2019

The Michelin Star

2019

The Michelin Star

Spice & Soul makes culinary history by receiving its first Michelin Star. Celebrated for our uncompromising flavor depth and artistic food plating, we gain recognition on the international gastronomic stage.

2022

Dual Accolades

2022

Dual Accolades

We are honored with our second Michelin Star. This achievement reflects our kitchen team's consistent precision and dedication to presenting traditional recipes in bold, innovative structures.

2024

Global Stage

2024

Global Stage

We mark our international debut in London's Mayfair district. Bringing single-origin spices and heirloom techniques to Europe, Spice & Soul begins its journey of representing luxury Indian heritage globally.
